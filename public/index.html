    Questions

    Is the score exact?

    No. It is an estimate. Use it to compare ideas, and open the links yourself to check.

    Where does my idea go?

    Your idea is sent to Google's Gemini AI and to paper and GitHub search. Your past checks stay only in your browser.

    Is it free?

    There is no sign-up. It uses your own free Gemini key, so the limits come from Google.

    Can it replace my project guide?

    No. It helps you talk to your guide with proof. Your guide decides what is allowed.

    IdeaForge-X
    IdeaForge-X is a student project tool. Results are estimates. They do not replace your guide's advice. A college version is planned for later. Built with Node.js, Express, the Gemini API, Semantic Scholar, arXiv and GitHub search.
