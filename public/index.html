    Questions

    How do students and teachers work together?

    Students register with their academic details and submit ideas. Teachers select the matching year, branch and section in Student groups. The teacher reviews ideas and can approve them or ask for changes. After approval, the team gets a roadmap, sends weekly progress reports, and receives teacher feedback in the project workspace. Students can also prepare literature summaries, a data sheet and review slides there.

    Can a team register together?

    Yes. One student can enter all team members in one registration. Each person has their own roll number, email and phone, and the whole group uses one shared password. Each member must verify their email.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Anyone can check an idea as a guest, up to 3 checks an hour. A guest's checks are not saved and cannot be sent to a teacher. Students need an account to submit ideas and use the project workspace. Teachers register with an email and a recovery question. A teacher who forgets a password can reset it by answering the recovery question; a student asks the teacher to reset it.

    Is the score exact?

    No. It is an estimate. Use it to compare ideas, and open the links yourself to check.

    Where does my idea go?

    Your idea is sent to Google's Gemini AI and to paper and GitHub search. Your account and your checks are saved on this site's server. If you choose Send to teacher, your teacher sees your name, roll number and report. Your password is stored scrambled, never as plain text.

    Is it free?

    Registering costs nothing. It uses the site owner's free Gemini key, so the limits come from Google.

    Can it replace my project guide?

    No. It helps you talk to your guide with proof. Your guide decides what is allowed.
