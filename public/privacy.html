IdeaForge-X

Privacy notice

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This notice explains what IdeaForge-X collects and why. The college or organization operating a hosted pilot should identify itself as the data fiduciary and provide its contact details before inviting students.

Information we process

  • Account details: name, email, roll number, phone number, year, branch, section, group name when students register together, role and password hashes (we do not store passwords in readable form). A group uses one shared password. Teacher accounts also include employee ID, department and designation.
  • Teacher sign-up details: name, email, employee ID, phone number, department, designation and recovery question answer. Email addresses are not verified during registration.
  • Project workspace details: idea descriptions, planned technology, AI check reports, weekly progress reports, evidence links, literature notes, data plans, teacher replies and review decisions.
  • Optional team profile: skills and interests, shown to opted-in classmates in the same student group.
  • Optional college archive entry: an idea description, technology and student group label appear after teacher approval only when every named team member has opted in. Any teammate can withdraw sharing, which removes the project from future archive searches.
  • Basic technical information such as request timestamps and an IP address held temporarily for abuse limits.

Why we use it

  • To create accounts, run idea checks, save reports and connect students with their teacher.
  • To provide project roadmaps, literature links, team suggestions and review materials.
  • To protect service availability and measure class or department level product impact.
  • To show an opted-in, approved project in that institution’s archive.

Service providers and disclosures

Idea checks may send the idea text and project context to Google Gemini. Search queries may be sent to public research, code, model and dataset search services, including OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, GitHub, Hugging Face, Kaggle and Devpost search. Google is used only when a person chooses Google sign-in and it has been configured. Password registration does not send email. The host may store account and project records in Firebase Firestore or local storage. These providers process data under their own terms. Do not put sensitive personal information in a project idea.

Sharing and retention

Your teacher can see projects you send, your identity, weekly updates, evidence links, literature and data worksheets, teacher replies and review decisions. College archive searches expose only opt-in, approved project descriptions, technology and student group label to students in the same institution. Team matches expose your name, roll number and skills only after you turn on matching. We keep account and project information while an account is active. Use Account to delete it; a teacher account deletion also removes its unassigned classes and student accounts, while shared college classes are transferred to another faculty member when possible. Backups, provider logs and records required to resolve security or legal issues may take time to expire.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw archive and team matching permissions, request access or correction, and delete your account through the account page. You may also contact your college administrator or the IdeaForge-X operator to exercise applicable rights or raise a complaint. A clear contact address must be configured by the operator for a production deployment.

Privacy contact: your college administrator.

This product notice is a starting point for a college pilot, not a certification of legal compliance. The organization operating the service must review the applicable Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, including their commencement timeline, and publish its actual identity, contact and grievance process.
